### Scenario results on synthetic fixtures

The fixtures (`fixtures/*.json`, generated by `scripts/make-fixtures.mjs`) model a 1280×720 face at 30 Hz with known yaw, distance, lip motion and lighting. They are **synthetic** and labelled as such.

| Fixture | Scripted event | Legible's behaviour | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| turning-away | 30° turn while talking, 10–14 s | Face nudge at **11.1 s**, cleared at **16.1 s**; 1 nudge | Nudge ≤ 1.5 s ✔ |
| silent-turning | 35° turn while listening, 8–20 s | **No nudge**; lights "Listening" | Never while listening ✔ |
| backlit | face/background 0.34, 10–25 s | Light nudge "Light is behind you" at **13.0 s** | ≤ 3 s after the 3 s hold rule, plus ≤ 0.5 s probe latency ✔ |
| rushing | 3.5 → 5.5 syllables/s at 25 s | Pace nudge at **31.6 s**; none before 25 s | Window rule (10 s) ✔ |
| covered-lens | no face, 10–15 s | Not tracking within **≤ 1 s**; no nudge; excluded from tracked talk | ≤ 1 s ✔ |
| second-face | `MULTIPLE_FACES_FOUND`, 10–16 s, while turned | Not tracking — more than one face (immediately); no nudge | ✔ |
| demo (100 s) | the whole story | Face → Light → Distance → Pace, nothing while listening | ✔ |
| rehearsal | 8 sentences | Baseline yaw 2.0°, rate within ±15% across two runs | ±15% ✔ |

**Receipt maths:** for every fixture, `trackedTalkMs` and `readableMs` equal a hand count over the tick frames, exactly (`tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts`). **Replay determinism:** the same fixture produces the same nudges on every run.

**Engine rules** (`tests/unit/engine.test.ts`): one nudge at a time by priority; clears after 2 s of good state; at most one new nudge per 5 s; no repeat within 30 s unless the previous one lasted more than 10 s; dropped at once when tracking is lost.

**Pauses (breathing trace, synthetic):** `long-run` (35 s of talking without a breath pause, then a breath every ~7 s) → "Pause between sentences" once at 25–27 s, then nothing; `demo` (breath pauses within 25 s) → Pauses light green, no nudge; fixtures without a breathing trace → "No data", never an issue (`tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts`). **Not yet run on a real chest trace.**

**Pace separability (synthetic):** 2.5 / 3.5 / 5 Hz lip motion gives rates that separate by more than 20% at each step. This still needs confirming on a real face (see `SDK-NOTES.md`).

**Hidden-window throttling:** with `backgroundThrottling: false`, the hidden sensor window's loop ran at **31 Hz** (shown in Panel → Now, asserted ≥ 25 Hz in e2e).

**Privacy (simulated):** no audio capture anywhere in the source; every `getUserMedia` passes `audio: false`; `requestInsight` is never called; telemetry is `false` in both SDK constructions; logs redact token-shaped strings; Delete everything leaves the data folder empty (e2e).

**Keyboard:** the welcome flow, the replay start, the panel tabs (arrow keys) and Escape to close the panel are covered in e2e. The strip's Tab order is lights (open panel) → Stop.

## Real-camera tests (22 Nov 2026)

**Setup:** HP Wide Vision HD Camera (0408:5482) at 1280×720, Windows 11 laptop (12 logical cores), one person at a desk, normal room light, SmartSpectra SDK 3.5.0. Run with `node scripts/live-check.mjs`: a rehearsal, then a scripted 3-minute live session with on-screen prompts (talk, turn ~15°, turn ~30°, turn away silently, cover the lens, lean back, talk fast/slow, room light off). The strip's state was logged every ~100 ms and a numbers-only recording was kept, so the same session can be replayed through the analyser (`tests/unit/live-recording.test.ts`).

The live session ran on the thresholds *before* tuning; it exposed false alarms (below). The thresholds were then tuned on this recording, and the **same recording replayed through the tuned rules** is shown in the last column.

| Test | Expected | Live, before tuning | Same session, tuned rules (replay) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initialization | Landmarks + talking ≤ 5 s | **5.3 s** from Start to tracking (SDK running at 5.3 s) | — |
| Readable while talking facing the camera | No nudge | ✗ "Move a little closer" at normal distance (mouth width 5.0–6.6% < 6% limit) | ✔ no nudge |
| Facing (turn ~30°+ to a second screen while talking) | Face nudge ≤ 1.5 s | ✔ Face nudge **0.5 s** after speech began while turned (yaw 35–58°) | ✔ same (49.5 s) |

**Rehearsal on a real face:** two runs; baseline yaw −3.0°, 23 s of talk measured. The practice receipt and "Rehearsal done" screen work (a bug that skipped "Rehearsal done" was found here and fixed).

**Changes made from this session** (all in `rules/thresholds.ts` unless noted, each with the measured numbers in a comment): speaking hangover 400 → 700 ms; hold timers survive pauses ≤ 1.5 s (`checks/evaluate.ts`); MULTIPLE_FACES_FOUND debounce 250 ms; backlight ratio 0.6 → 0.42; distance mouth width 6% → 3.5%; Pace off by default; the SDK readiness check no longer runs before sessions (it took ~24 s and once returned `blocked` because nobody was seated yet).

**Caveat:** one person, one camera, one room, one session. These thresholds are a first real-data tuning, not a validated setting.

## Usability and benefit test (protocol, not yet run)

**Part A, usability.** Five hearing participants run Rehearsal unaided, then a 10-minute call with a friend. Note whether each nudge was understood and whether it distracted. Pass: 4 of 5 understand every nudge they saw; message read in under 1 s at 70 cm.

**Part B, benefit.** Each participant reads 10 short sentences twice: once with typical habits (glancing at a second screen, a window behind them), and once after Rehearsal with Legible running. Each run is recorded with their own OS camera app and their consent, outside Legible (Legible itself records nothing). Four or five viewers, ideally including one deaf or hard-of-hearing lip-reader, watch the clips muted, in random order, and write down the words. Compare word accuracy between the two conditions. Report it as a small relative comparison, not as proof.
